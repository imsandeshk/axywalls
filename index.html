1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, AXY Walls may process:

  • Basic Google account details, such as name, email, profile picture and account identifier, when you sign in.
  • Purchase and entitlement information needed to verify PRO access.
  • Notification preferences and push tokens, if applicable.
  • Wallpaper preferences, favorites and app settings.
  • Technical information necessary for app functionality and troubleshooting, where applicable.

2. Google Sign-In

AXY Walls supports Google Sign-In to identify your account and manage PRO features.

Google authentication may provide your name, email address, profile picture and unique account identifier.

We use this information for authentication, associating purchases with your account and restoring eligible PRO access.

We do not receive or store your Google account password.

3. PRO Verification and Purchases

AXY Walls may offer premium wallpapers and PRO features through Google Play Billing.

Purchase identifiers, transaction status, product identifiers and entitlement records may be processed to:

  • Verify purchases and activate PRO features.
  • Restore eligible purchases.
  • Prevent unauthorized access to premium content.
  • Update access when purchases are refunded, canceled or revoked.

Payments are processed by Google Play. We do not directly receive full payment card details.

4. Notifications

AXY Walls may request notification permission to send new wallpaper alerts, feature updates and important service information.

You can disable notifications through Android settings. If push notifications are implemented, a device registration token may be processed for delivery.

5. App Permissions

  • Internet: Loading wallpapers, signing in and verifying PRO status.
  • Notifications: Delivering permitted application notifications.
  • Photos or media: Saving, importing or using wallpaper images where required.
  • Wallpaper functionality: Setting home-screen or lock-screen wallpapers using supported Android capabilities.

Permissions are used for their stated purposes. Some features may be unavailable when relevant permissions are denied.

6. How We Use Information

Information may be used to operate the app, authenticate users, verify PRO purchases, maintain preferences, deliver requested features, send permitted notifications, resolve technical issues and protect against misuse.
